Photography

A world-building eye. I photograph the small terrains most people walk across: the way water draws on sand, how grass remembers wind, the marks people leave on shared ground. These studies feed straight back into how I think about texture, pace and space in my design work.

Cyanotypes

Cyanotype on paper · Eucalyptus and West Coast plants

Cyanotypes of eucalyptus and vegetation from the west coast of Canada. Each print is made by laying the plants on light-sensitive paper in the sun: wherever a leaf blocks the light, the paper stays white.
