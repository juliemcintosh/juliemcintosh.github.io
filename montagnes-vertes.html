An installation proposal for Annecy Paysages · with Andrea McIntosh

Montagnes vertes

Six pine structures that trace the outline of the Franco-Swiss mountains, with pots of local wildflowers hidden in their slopes.

Location
Parc Charles Bosson, Annecy (proposed)
Context
Appel à candidature Jeune Création, Annecy Paysages 2022
Status
Proposal, 2022
Project type
Installation proposal
Role
Co-designer
Collaborators
Andrea McIntosh, architecture
Budget
≈ €3,000

The project

The design celebrates the mountains of Haute-Savoie. Six complementary but separate pine structures outline the Franco-Swiss Alps, held together by dowels and mitred half-lap joints. Hidden in their slopes are pots of wildflowers that grow on the very mountains around Lake Annecy.

The structures invite people to come up close and learn about the wild plants that can be safely grown in an urban environment. Building on Franco-Swiss research into reintroducing local plants, the proposal brings art, sustainability and learning together to encourage conversation and a deeper appreciation for biodiversity.

How it was made

Terracotta planters suspended by dowels would hold wildflowers of the region: rhododendrons, myrtilles, yellow and blue gentians, the Neydens tulip, mountain arnica and blue thistle, all growing seasonally with little maintenance.

The design was built around the Fleurs locales program, three years of research by France Nature Environnement Haute-Savoie into regrowing local wildflowers in the Franco-Swiss regions. Their education lead confirmed that growing and sourcing these wildflowers was achievable, and the proposal included working with FNE to establish an accessible wildflower garden for visitors and locals alike.

The team

Andrea and Julie McIntosh are francophone sisters from Sudbury, Ontario, whose work explores the relationship between people, tradition and nature. At the time, Andrea was completing her Master of Architecture at Carleton University and Julie was finishing Aboriginal Visual Arts at the New Brunswick College of Craft and Design.
